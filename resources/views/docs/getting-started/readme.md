# Features

Vidra is a modern, scalable, and modular school management system designed to simplify educational institution operations.

## Core Features

- 🎓 **Student Management** – Manage student profiles, admissions, records, and academic details.
- 👨‍🏫 **Teacher Management** – Add and manage teachers, departments, and staff information.
- 🏫 **Multi-Branch Support** – Centralized management for schools with multiple branches.
- 🔐 **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)** – Fine-grained permissions for Admin, Teacher, Staff, Student, and custom roles.
- 📚 **Class & Subject Management** – Create classes, sections, subjects, and assign teachers.
- 📝 **Attendance Management** – Track student and staff attendance efficiently.
- 💰 **Fee Management** – Manage fee structures, payments, and financial tracking.
- 📊 **Dashboard & Analytics** – Get insights into school performance and operations.
- 📅 **Academic Session Management** – Easily manage academic years and sessions.
- 📄 **Exam & Result Management** – Conduct exams and manage report cards.
- 📢 **Notice & Announcement System** – Share important updates with students and staff.
- 📥 **Import & Export Support** – Easily manage data through Excel/CSV.
- 🔍 **Advanced Search & Filters** – Quickly find records across modules.
- 🌐 **Responsive UI** – Works seamlessly on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.

## Modular Architecture

Vidra supports a **bundle/plugin system**, allowing schools to extend functionality without modifying core code.

Available premium or optional modules may include:

- 📖 Library Management
- 🎓 Alumni Management
- 🚌 Transport Management
- 🏥 Hostel Management
- 💬 Communication Tools
- 📱 SMS & Notification Integration
