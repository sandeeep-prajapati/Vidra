# Student Management

Manage the **complete student lifecycle** — from initial registration with personal and admission details to filtering, searching, and viewing individual student profiles.

---

## Features

- **Student List** — browse all students with filters
- **Add Student** — register a new student with full details
- **Admission Info** — admission number and date
- **Parent/Guardian Info** — father and mother contact details
- **Status Management** — Active, Inactive, Graduated

---

## Student List

Navigate to `/students` to browse all registered students.

### Filters

Filter by **Status** (`Active`, `Inactive`, `Graduated`) or search by **Student Name**. Click **Filter** to apply, **Reset** to clear.

---

## Add a Student

### Step 1 — Personal Details

1. Click **Add Student**
2. Fill in:
   - **First Name** and **Last Name**
   - **Date of Birth** — e.g. `2012-06-15`
   - **Gender** — `Male`, `Female`, or `Other`
   - **Phone Number** — student or guardian contact
   - **Email** — student email
   - **Nationality** — e.g. `Indian`
   - **Current Address** — where the student currently lives
   - **Permanent Address** — permanent/home address

### Step 2 — Admission Details

   - **Admission Number** — unique admission ID (e.g. `ADM-2026-001`)
   - **Date of Admission** — date the student enrolled

### Step 3 — Parent / Guardian Details

   - **Father's Name** — full name of father
   - **Father's Phone** — father's contact number
   - **Mother's Name** — full name of mother
   - **Mother's Phone** — mother's contact number

3. Click **Save Student**

---

## Student Profiles

Each student has a profile page that shows:

- Personal and admission details
- Parent contacts
- Linked attendance records
- Assigned batch and class
- Fee history

---

## Tips

- The **Admission Number** must be unique — use a consistent format (e.g. `ADM-YYYY-####`).
- Mark students as **Inactive** when they leave, rather than deleting — historical records are preserved.
- A student must be added here before they can be assigned to a batch, fee structure, or exam schedule.
