# Timetable Management

Manage academic schedules, classrooms, teaching periods, substitute teachers, and school events from one centralized system.

The **Timetable Management Module** helps schools organize classes efficiently while avoiding scheduling conflicts between teachers, rooms, and periods.

---

## Features

- Class Timetable Management
- Room Management
- Day & Period Scheduling
- Subject & Teacher Assignment
- Substitute Teacher Management
- Special Event Scheduling
- Class-wise Timetable View

---
