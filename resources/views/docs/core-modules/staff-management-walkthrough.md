# Staff Management

Manage **all teaching and non-teaching staff** in one place — from adding new staff members with personal and employment details to filtering and searching existing records.

---

## Features

- **Staff List** — browse all staff with filter and search
- **Add Staff** — register a new staff member
- **Employment Details** — track department, designation, and employment type
- **Status Management** — mark staff as Active or Inactive

---

## Staff List

Navigate to `/staff` to see all registered staff members.

### Filters

Filter by **Status** (`Active`, `Inactive`), **Department**, or **Employment Type** (`Permanent`, `Contract`, `Part-time`).

Click **Filter** to apply filters, **Reset** to clear them.

---

## Add a Staff Member

### Step 1 — Personal Details

1. Click **Add Staff**
2. Fill in the personal information:
   - **First Name** and **Last Name**
   - **Date of Birth** — e.g. `1990-03-20`
   - **Gender** — `Male`, `Female`, or `Other`
   - **Phone Number** — contact number
   - **Email** — staff email address
   - **Nationality** — e.g. `Indian`
   - **Address** — full residential address

### Step 2 — Employment Details

Continue filling in employment information:

   - **Joining Date** — start date of employment
   - **Designation** — job title (e.g. `Teacher`, `Principal`)
   - **Employment Type** — `Permanent`, `Contract`, or `Part-time`
   - **Status** — `Active` or `Inactive`

3. Click **Save Staff Member**

---

## Staff Profiles

After saving, each staff member gets a profile page where you can:

- View and edit all personal and employment details
- Track attendance records linked to this staff member
- View assigned subjects (if a teacher)

---

## Tips

- Use the **Status** filter to quickly see who is currently employed.
- The **Joining Date** is used to calculate service years in reports.
- Staff members flagged as `Inactive` remain in the system for historical records.
- Teachers must be added here before they can be assigned to subjects or timetable entries.
